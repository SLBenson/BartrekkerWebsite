Useful Travel Apps To Make Travelling More Interesting

14 September 2026 • 10 min read • By the Bartrekker Team

Let’s be honest, planning a trip can feel tedious, especially when you are on a tight schedule with lots to do or you are travelling with a group of people and want to keep everything organised so the holiday doesn’t break up friendships.

If you want to avoid the awkward “where are we going?” group chats, last minute packing panic, figuring out your roaming data so you don’t have to take out a second mortgage for a phone bill or just generally want some order, then fortunately your phone can do the heavy lifting.

If you download the right applications before you ever start planning, you can make your trip look like it was planned by a professional, kick your feet up and enjoy it for what it is, a break, not a job.

Here are 12 essential apps to help you beat the holiday stress:

Plan Your Trip With Ease

Stippl

Tired of clicking through endless browser tabs and losing the will to live? Stippl has got you with a number of extremely powerful tools you can kiss the messy planning goodbye with:

  • The AI itinerary planner is useful for tailored experiences and routes, just describe your dream trip and it can build full day-by-day plans that can be edited.
  • A drag and drop map planner that can sync your accommodation, budgets and routes in real time.
  • Use the discovery tab to find curated routes made by other travellers and influencers then tweak them to fit your needs.

Packpoint

Is packing a pain for you too? Endless weather research, trying to plan outfits for 20 different occasions and possibilities, Packpoint has got you covered with:

  • Packpoint will give you a tailored list of items to pack based on a number of factors; your length of stay, weather in your destination over that time and any activities you have planned.
  • Working in harmony with Stippl, this pairing is the perfect recipe for a smooth pre-trip routine.

Get Connected When You Arrive

Airalo

Have you ever been slapped with a large data roaming bill or even worse, get a notification that you are being charged whilst you are still away?It's one way to ruin the holiday mood. Airalo can help you prevent that with an e-sim, make sure your phone is compatible and you can have:

  • Flexible data packages with some including calls and texts, manage it all through the app and get prepared for when you arrive at your destination so you don’t have any downtime from being connected.
  • Perfect for that awkward time just after you land when you need your data the most.

DeepL

Do standard translators sometimes let you down? Sometimes things get lost in translation and can make communication or understanding awkward. DeepL is attempting to break that language barrier with:

  • Helping you navigate foreign language barriers with contextual awareness and interpretation not just literal word-for-word translation to make sure there’s no messy communication mishaps.
  • Real time voice translation and instant camera translation to keep communication easy on the go.

Figure Out Where To Go And Navigate Like A Pro

Rome2rio

Getting lost is only romantic in films. In reality, it usually ends up with sore feet, defeat and tired arguments. Rome2rio can guide you in the right direction no matter how obscure the route:

  • Solve complex transport logistics between two points suggesting a variety of route options and transport methods that can suit your needs.
  • Simultaneously map out and compare the cost of flights, trains, buses, ferries and driving routes to curate the best way from A to B or even C and D.

Spotted by locals

Do you ever find yourself saying “there are too many tourists” or “this place is too touristy”? Most people fall into the tourist traps when visiting popular spots, if you want to avoid that and find where the locals go Spotted by locals can:

  • Give you curated city guides written strictly by verified, non-commercial, local residents. Helping you avoid the crowds and push you to support neighbourhood spots.
  • Turn on the app’s proximity filter to find hidden gems that you would usually miss.

Food Is Always Important, So Eat Like A Local

World of Mouth

You are usually trying to cram as many good meals into a short time when you are on holiday, one bad meal can break a perfect trip, use World of Mouth to:

  • Source highly vetted, expert-backed recommendations from high quality food reviewers, chefs and sommeliers. Don’t worry about running into fake reviews and spam.
  • Keep a dining diary of the places you eat to remember for future.

Too Good To Go

On a budget? Too good to go has been a well respected app that helps people around the world find high quality food on a budget whilst helping reduce environmental waste by:

  • Connecting you with local bakeries, restaurants, cafes and hotels selling surplus inventory as surprise bags, you never know what you are going to get, but this is part of the fun.
  • Sending you push notifications for the best spots in town to make sure you are well informed of the latest deals.

Manage Your Spending Efficiently

Splitwise

If there is one thing that causes disputes on a group holiday it’s money, who paid for this and who paid for that. Tracking spending for a group has always been an issue until now with Splitwise:

  • Track group spending by person, a shared group pot that shows exactly what people paid for in over 150 currencies. Splitwise allows you to upload and share bills to make splitting costs easier.
  • Settling up is made simple, it collates all expenses and tidies up the numbers to make it easier to know who owes what and to who.
  • Cheeky tip - in the free version have different group members enter expenses to stay below the daily entry limits.

ATM fee saver

It’s difficult to know exactly how much money you need to exchange when you are taking a trip. Tired of being charged ridiculous fees to take out your cash abroad? ATM fee saver could help you save a little extra money:

  • Finds fee-free or low-fee cash machines abroad and helps you avoid bad conversion rates when exchanging.
  • Operates in over 180 countries and features a guide to avoid Dynamic Currency Conversion to keep your bank from overcharging you.

Record Your Trip every step of the way

Polarsteps

Holiday pictures usually get taken and then fall down your camera roll never to be seen again, which is a shame because there are most likely some great memories there, Polarsteps helps you turn your travel route into a keepsake:

  • Automate route mapping that doesn’t drain your phone battery, tracking your travel path, photos you take and milestones onto an interactive timeline.
  • Share your interactive trip link with family and friends at home so they can follow your journey in real time without needing constant updates.

Journi

Holiday memories tend to stay digital nowadays, gone are the days when we had physical pictures locked away to go back to and reminisce. Journi is bringing this back:

  • Giving you the chance to turn your digital travel photos into premium, physical keepsakes with easy to use formatting.
  • Uses built in AI layout tools to instantly design custom photobooks using your phone's photo metadata. With permission the app can pull photo location data to automatically insert custom maps and stamps onto your pages.
  • Create high-quality hardback themed photobooks, calendars and more.

These solutions are not your typical group of applications that you would usually get suggested, however they all contribute something unique to make your holiday that much more enjoyable.
